Projects

Things I’ve built with AI.

The same instinct that runs through the day job — hand the repetitive work to a system, keep the judgment for yourself — applied at home. Each of these started as something for the family, and each one runs on its own now: new books every night, new sketches twice a day, a playable game, a live map of the sky.

  1. 01

    Colin & Maddy’s Storybook Library

    A new picture book for my kids every night — written, illustrated, and narrated with AI, then published to a library site where they can pick a book and press Listen for read-aloud narration. 80+ books and counting.

    Visit the library →

  2. 02

    Colin’s Truck Coloring Pages

    An ever-growing archive of AI-drawn coloring pages for my son — fire trucks, tow trucks, monster trucks, and more, each with its own scene. New sketches are generated twice a day, and every page can be downloaded and printed.

    Browse the coloring pages →

  3. 03

    Fire Truck Simulator 3D

    Drive Engine 7 around a 3D town as Captain Alex, answer Dispatcher Rosa's emergency calls, and put out fires with a mouse-aimed water hose. Eleven missions plus Free Roam, four trucks to drive — pumper, ladder, brush pickup, and command SUV — with realistic sirens and engine sounds. Playable in the browser with keyboard and mouse.

    Play the game →

  4. 04

    Earth Satellites

    A real-time 3D Earth you can spin and zoom, surrounded by all 15,860 active satellites — from the ISS and Hubble to the full Starlink constellation. Click any satellite for its altitude, speed, and orbit path, or hide them all to see the continents on a daylight map.

    Explore the satellites →
