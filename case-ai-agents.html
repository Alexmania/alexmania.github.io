Case Study 03 — Meta / AI Automation

AI agents for intake triage

Recurring manual work handed to agents, so people spend their time on the requests that actually need a person.

Context

Partner intake for the Meta Account platform, built by consolidating two separate processes into one system with scoped request forms and impacted-surface capture — as the platform scaled toward 30+ integrated apps.

Problem

Manual triage took 15–30 minutes per request, and requests arrived across six product surfaces. High-volume intake and defect inflow kept growing, and adding headcount was not the answer.

Solution

Alex deployed multiple agents that eliminated recurring manual work: an intake triage agent that auto-resolves the majority of incoming requests, and a content-based bug-classification agent covering 18 product categories.

Wins

Handling time fell from 15–30 minutes to under 30 seconds, with 45+ requests triaged across six product surfaces — scaling high-volume intake and defect inflow without added headcount.

The Vibes app launch is a separate entry on the Experience page, not part of this case study.
