Case Study 02 — Meta / Design System

Design-system adoption

Adoption is a program, not a library — partner teams came aboard because the system made their work easier.

Context

A cross-app design system at Meta, starting from zero adopted products. Alex ran roadmapping, capacity planning, and sprint planning across 5 teams — prioritizing work, delivering components and frameworks, providing technical support, and giving leadership clear visibility into priorities and resource allocation.

Problem

Adoption is a coordination problem, not a component problem. A library nobody uses is not a system — the work was convincing partner teams, one by one, that adopting it was worth their time.

Solution

A deliberate adoption program: launching the first 5 onboarded products and proving benefits and efficiencies to partner teams, so the next teams had evidence, not just a pitch.

Wins

Adoption grew from 0 to 70+ products in under 2 years.
