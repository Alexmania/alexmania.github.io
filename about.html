About

I focus on building simple, scalable experiences.

I’m a Technical Program Manager (TPM) based in the Pacific Northwest, where I live with my wife, son, and daughter.

In my professional work, I focus on building and scaling user-centric experiences, with a particular passion for mobile technology and cross-functional collaboration. I enjoy partnering closely with engineering and product teams to ensure alignment on priorities and to deliver solutions that make a meaningful impact for end users. Seeing products I’ve contributed to reach millions of people continues to be a key motivator in my career.

Operating principles

  1. Build simple, scalable experiences

    I focus on building simple, scalable experiences — and I hold that bar no matter how big the program gets.

  2. Partner closely, align on priorities

    I partner closely with engineering and product teams, and I make sure we’re aligned on priorities before the work starts.

  3. Deliver for the end user

    The test of my work is whether it makes a meaningful impact for the people who use it.

  4. Automate the repetitive

    When work repeats, I look for a way a system can do it — so people can spend their time where judgment matters.

  5. Keep learning, keep improving

    I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, improve, and take on challenges that expand my perspective.

Beyond work

Outside of work, I’ve embarked on a journey to live a healthier and more balanced life. I began running in the summer of 2025 and have since completed my first marathon — and I continue to enjoy running. This has also sparked a deeper interest in holistic health, where I’m learning more about how fitness, nutrition, and overall well-being contribute to long-term growth and resilience.

Beyond running, I enjoy spending time with my family, exploring the outdoors, and traveling to new destinations. Whether it’s through my professional projects or personal pursuits, I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, improve, and take on challenges that expand my perspective.
