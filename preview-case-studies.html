Case Studies

A few programs that show how I work: navigating ambiguity, aligning large cross-functional groups, and shipping complex efforts to real users.

Centralizing user settings across Meta's family of apps

Technical Program Manager, Growth • Core Product — Meta
4 concurrent programs 2B+ users reached 5 teams aligned Facebook • Instagram • Oculus • WhatsApp

The challenge

User settings lived in four different apps, each with its own teams, roadmaps, and technical constraints. Centralizing them meant coordinating parallel workstreams across Facebook, Instagram, Oculus, and WhatsApp — without breaking the release trains of products used by billions of people.

What I owned

  • Created the intake process and program roadmaps, giving every incoming request a clear path from proposal to prioritized plan.
  • Unified capacity planning across 5 teams so dependencies surfaced early instead of at integration time.
  • Drove cross-app alignment on priorities, sequencing, and launch readiness across engineering, product, and design.

Outcome

Shipped the first 5 products onto a new cross-app design system and delivered centralized settings experiences to a global audience of 2B+ users — with the intake and planning machinery in place to keep the program running.

Taking a design system from 0 to 70+ products

Technical Program Manager — Meta
0 → 70+ products Under 2 years VR • Web • Native stacks

The challenge

A new cross-app design system is only as good as its adoption. Partner teams had working solutions and little incentive to migrate — the system had to earn its place on technical merit and proven value, across VR, web, and native stacks.

What I owned

  • Partnered with teams to demonstrate concrete efficiency and quality benefits of adopting the system, rather than mandating it.
  • Supported adoption across multiple tech stacks, removing the integration blockers unique to each platform.
  • Tracked and communicated adoption progress to build momentum — early wins made the next teams easier to bring on board.

Outcome

Drove adoption from zero to 70+ products in under two years, establishing the design system as the default foundation for cross-app experiences.

Large-scale retail migration & global compliance at Amazon

Technical Program Manager, Customer Delivery Experience — Amazon
10+ external teams 100+ teams via intake Global tax & consumer-protection compliance

The challenge

Migrating some of the most-trafficked retail pages on the internet while simultaneously meeting global tax and consumer-protection compliance requirements — with over a hundred teams interfacing through a single intake process.

What I owned

  • Led the large-scale migration effort, coordinating with 10+ external teams on sequencing, dependencies, and cutover.
  • Owned the global tax & consumer-protection compliance effort end to end.
  • Designed an API/service to surface complete order-total breakdowns consistently across retail pages.
  • Managed the intake process interfacing with 100+ teams, keeping requests triaged, prioritized, and visible.

Outcome

Delivered the migration and the compliance program on track, with a shared API for order-total transparency and an intake process that scaled to triple-digit team participation.
